Create a beautiful, modern, responsive website for my personal long-term journey to become a professional singer, musician, recording artist, producer, mixing engineer and mastering engineer.

The website should feel like a premium personal music academy and learning dashboard — NOT like a generic online course marketplace.

## PROJECT NAME

# CLASS 1 CONTENT

Populate Class 1 with:

Title:
"Meet Your Voice"

Objective:
"Understand your natural speaking and singing voice and establish your Day-1 baseline."

Lesson:

1. Natural speaking voice
2. Natural singing voice
3. Listening without judging
4. Identifying strengths
5. Identifying weaknesses
6. Creating a baseline recording

Assignment:

Record:
- 20 seconds of natural speech
- 20–30 seconds of natural singing

Then answer:

1. What did I notice about my voice?
2. Where did I feel comfortable?
3. Where did I struggle?
4. Did I run out of breath?
5. Did I notice pitch problems?
6. What do I currently consider my biggest weakness?
7. What do I think is my strongest point?

Completion requirement:

Upload recording
Complete reflection
Mark assignment complete
